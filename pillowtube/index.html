PillowTube

A gadget to help you shut out the noise and protect your brain on YouTube.

PillowTube is a Safari extension for Mac, iPhone, and iPad. Choose what to see and change how you browse and watch YouTube in Safari, not in the YouTube app.

What you can change

  • Hide distractions. Choose whether to show Shorts, comments, and recommendations.
  • Skip the detours. Open Subscriptions instead of Home, or go straight to a channel’s Videos tab.
  • Tweak playback and layout. Turn off autoplay or video previews. On Mac, show Home and Subscriptions as lists and adjust the content width.

Get started

Open the PillowTube app for setup instructions. Enable PillowTube in Safari’s extension settings and allow access to YouTube. Then open YouTube in Safari and use the PillowTube extension popup to choose what to show.
