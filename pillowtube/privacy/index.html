PillowTube Privacy Policy

This policy covers the PillowTube app and its Safari extension on Mac, iPhone, and iPad. You do not need a PillowTube account. The app and extension do not send your browsing activity or settings to the developer. They do not include analytics, advertising trackers, or crash reporting.

Access to YouTube pages

When you allow access in Safari, the extension reads parts of the YouTube pages you visit, including page URLs, links, visible labels, and video player state. It uses that information on your device to recognize page features and apply your settings. It runs on www.youtube.com, m.youtube.com, and channel pages on studio.youtube.com. It does not save a browsing history or send the page information it reads to the developer or another service.

Some optional settings open a different page on YouTube, such as Subscriptions instead of Home. Safari loads those pages normally. YouTube's own handling of your visits, account activity, and cookies is covered by Google's Privacy Policy, not this policy.

Information used on your device

Safari stores your PillowTube preferences in local extension storage. They remain there between sessions until you change them or Safari clears that storage. PillowTube does not sync preferences between devices. The extension checks browser and device information to adapt its controls, and uses screen width when you apply a desktop layout preset. It does not store the raw device information or screen width. On Mac, the setup app checks whether the extension is enabled so it can show that status to you.

You can change your preferences or restore defaults for available options in the extension popup. To stop the extension from accessing YouTube, revoke its website access or disable it in Safari settings. There is no developer-held copy of your PillowTube preferences or browsing activity to delete.

Contact

For privacy questions or to request deletion of information you have sent directly to the developer, email damjan.dev@icloud.com. If you contact the developer, the details you choose to share are separate from the data the app and extension handle on your device.
